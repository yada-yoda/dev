Lisa Rinna, Harry Hamlin, Andrew Rannells, Tituss Burgess, Megan Hilty to star in ‘It’s a Wonderful Life’ on Broadway this winter

Harry Hamlin American actorAndrew Rannells American actorLisa Rinna actor, writer

It’s A Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play is set to make its Broadway premiere this holiday season. Directed by Tony Award winner Jerry Zaks, it stars Andrew Rannells (Gutenberg!), Megan Hilty (Death Becomes Her), Tituss Burgess (Oh, Mary!), Harry Hamlin (Anne Rice’s Mayfair Witches) and Lisa Rinna (Days of Our Lives). — Deadline
