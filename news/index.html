    How this works

    Free trade feeds are read twice a day. A venue named in a headline gives a story its city; the phrasing gives it a type; names are checked against Wikidata. Anything the rules are unsure about is held back rather than guessed at, so this is not everything, only what could be filed with confidence. Headlines and photographs belong to the outlets, which are credited and linked on every story.
