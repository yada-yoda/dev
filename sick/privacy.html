TL;DR

Illness Tracker is a free personal notebook for tracking your own health observations. Your data lives in your browser (or your own Google account if you sign in). Nobody else — including the developer — can read it. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers. This app is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Only what you type in: illness episodes, symptoms, dates, severity, temperature, oxygen saturation (SpO2), medicines, dosages, dose intervals, locations, activities, contacts (names you enter), notes, and diagnoses. Nothing is scraped from your device, inferred from your behavior, or collected without you actively logging it.

Where it's stored

  • In your browser (localStorage) by default. This is on your device only and never leaves it unless you sign in.
  • In Google Firestore (Firebase project illnesstracker-8d888) if you sign in with Google. Your data is stored under /users/{your-google-uid}. Firestore security rules enforce that only you can read or write to that path.

Who can access it

  • You — always.
  • Not the developer, not third parties, not advertisers. There are no analytics, no trackers, no ad networks.
  • Google, as the storage provider, has the same technical access to their servers that they always do. They are not given access to interpret or share your data content.

Third-party assets

The page loads a few third-party resources for functionality:

  • Firebase (auth + Firestore) — only when you sign in
  • Google Fonts (DM Sans + JetBrains Mono) — served by fonts.gstatic.com
  • html2canvas (from cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — loaded on demand when you export a PNG

These providers may see your IP address as part of normal HTTP traffic. They are not sent any of your illness data.

Google Calendar integration

If you click "Sync to Google Calendar", the app asks Google for the calendar.events OAuth scope. This lets it push illness events to your calendar as reminders/records. It does not read your existing calendar. You can revoke this permission any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cookies & tracking

None. No cookies (Firebase auth uses IndexedDB, not cookies), no analytics, no ad networks, no fingerprinting.

Data export & deletion

  • Export everything — click 💾 Backup for a full JSON file, or 📄 PDF for a formatted report.
  • Delete local data — clear your browser's site data for this domain.
  • Delete cloud data — while signed in, delete individual entries via the History tab. To wipe your Firestore doc entirely, revoke the app under Google Account permissions and contact the developer if residual data cleanup is needed.

Medical Disclaimer

This app is not a medical device. It cannot diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

What this app IS

  • A personal notebook for logging symptoms, medicines, and vital signs you record yourself
  • A pattern viewer that surfaces recurring locations, contacts, and activities from your own logged data
  • A calendar view of your own health history

What this app is NOT

  • A diagnostic tool
  • A source of medical advice
  • A replacement for a healthcare provider
  • FDA / CE cleared or reviewed by any regulatory body
  • Guaranteed to be accurate, complete, or fit for medical use

In an emergency

Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number. Do not use this app during a medical emergency. It is not designed to alert anyone or contact emergency services.

Before making health decisions

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional. The Risk Factors and pattern analysis in this app are simple statistical heuristics based only on data you have entered — they are not clinical assessments. A recurring location or contact does not imply a causal relationship.

Medicine tracking

Dose timers and interval reminders are calculated from what you type in. They can be wrong if the input is wrong. Always follow the label on the medicine and the guidance of your prescriber. This app cannot detect dangerous drug interactions, allergies, contraindications, or overdoses. If in doubt about a dose or interaction, call a pharmacist or your doctor before taking the medicine.

Temperature severity colors

The color-coded temperature ranges (blue → green → yellow → orange → red) are common consumer references. They are not clinical fever thresholds and vary by age, pregnancy, medications, and individual baseline. Infants under 3 months in particular have different thresholds — consult a pediatrician for any fever in that age group.

SpO2 tracking

Consumer pulse oximeters can be inaccurate, especially in low perfusion, cold fingers, dark nail polish, or people with darker skin tones. If you're using SpO2 to monitor a serious illness, do not rely on this app or any single reading — get medical care.

Data-driven guidance

Any suggestion, correlation, or "insight" surfaced by the app is derived only from data you have entered. Small sample sizes produce noisy patterns. Correlation is not causation. Use the analysis as a personal journaling aid, not as clinical evidence.

By using this app, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer, and that you will consult a qualified healthcare provider before making any decisions about your health.
