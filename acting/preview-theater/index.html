Theater Venue Annotation

Three ways to handle the "All shows at The Second City Training Center, Chicago" disclaimer that future-proof for credits at other venues. Each option is rendered with the actual PDF print CSS so what you see is what you'll get in the exported PDF. Pick one and Claude ships it.

A Asterisk + footnote at bottom (your suggestion · recommended)

Stage names that are Second City get a small superscript asterisk. A single dim footnote at the bottom of the section maps that asterisk to the parent venue. Non-Second City credits simply don't get an asterisk and aren't covered by the footnote.

B Inline parent venue per row

Each venue cell shows the stage on the first line and the parent venue dim and small underneath. No shared footnote needed — every row carries its own context. Adds one extra line of height per credit.

· How to pick

Quick read:
  • Option A — best for future-proofing. Standard convention. Recommended unless you have a strong preference for something else.
  • Option B — most context-rich but takes a noticeable chunk of vertical space, which costs you breathing room elsewhere on the one-page PDF.
  • Option C — leanest. Works if you're confident your readers know the room names.
How Decap will work after pick:
  • Option A: each Theater entry gets a new boolean field "Second City stage?" (default ON). Uncheck for any non-SC future credit. The footnote at the bottom auto-hides if no entry has the box checked.
  • Option B: each Theater entry gets a new optional "Parent venue" string field. Type "The Second City" for SC stages, the actual house for others, or leave blank for a single-line row.
  • Option C: no schema change. Decap stays as-is.
