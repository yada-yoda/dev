Privacy Policy, Terms of Use & Disclaimer

Effective July 10, 2026. Clover is a private, single-user personal-finance tracker. This page explains what data it touches, how it's stored, and the terms under which it's provided.

Privacy Policy

What data Clover stores, and where

  • The financial information you enter (income, expenses, bills, paychecks, accounts, and similar records) is stored in a Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database belonging to the app's owner. Access is restricted by security rules to the single authorized account — the data is never public and never listed anywhere.
  • Some display preferences (column layouts, collapsed panels, analytics opt-out) are stored locally in your browser's storage and never leave your device.

Sign-in

Clover uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Clover never sees or stores your password; it receives only your Google account's basic profile (name, email, photo) to identify you. Only the pre-authorized account can read or write data.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Clover requests calendar access to create and maintain one dedicated calendar and push events to it (paychecks, bills, CD maturities). The sync is strictly one-way: Clover never reads your existing calendars or events. The access token stays in your browser session and is not stored on any server. You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the created calendar freely.

Third-party requests

  • Public bank rate feeds (e.g. a bank's published products endpoint) may be fetched read-only to record advertised savings rates. No personal data is sent with these requests.
  • Google Analytics measures anonymous page usage (no financial data is ever sent to it). You can opt out on any visit by adding ?ga=off to the address.
  • Charting and CSV libraries load from public CDNs; they receive no personal data.

What Clover never does

  • Never sells, shares, or transmits your financial data to anyone.
  • Never connects to your bank accounts or moves money — every record is entered or imported by you.
  • Never stores credentials, card numbers, or government identifiers.

Deletion

You can export a full backup at any time (Import / Export → Download backup) and delete any or all records in the app. Deleting data removes it from the Firestore database.
