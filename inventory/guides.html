eBay

Largest US marketplace

Just about anything sells here — collectibles, vintage, electronics, clothing. Supports both fixed-price and auction. Best discoverability of any platform but the most fees.

Title
80 chars
Photos
12 free, 1600×1600+ for zoom
Fees (2026)
~13.25% FVF + $0.30/order; insertion $0.35 after 250 free/mo
Format
Auction / Fixed Price / GTC

From The Ledger

Export → eBay File Exchange (CSV) generates an upload-ready file: Action, CustomLabel (SKU), Title, ConditionID, Description, dimensions, weight, brand, UPC, year — all mapped from your item.

Quick tips

  • Pack keywords into the title — skip filler ("L@@K", "RARE"). Search ranking weights title heavily.
  • Free returns boost your placement in eBay search.
  • Promoted Listings can move slow items but cut into margin — start at 2% ad rate.
  • Use UPC where applicable — eBay auto-fills product details and bumps SEO.
  • For Beanies: include tag generation, condition, and any errors in the title (e.g. "PVC PE" matters).

Poshmark

Social, clothing-leaning

Closet-style marketplace built around social discovery. Stronger for clothing and accessories, but Beanies and small collectibles do well too. Built-in prepaid shipping label means no shipping logistics for you.

Title
50 chars (Ledger flags titles over this)
Photos
1 cover + 7 detail = 8 total
Fees
20% on sales ≥ $15, flat $2.95 below
Shipping
Poshmark handles it; buyer pays $7.97 USPS Priority

From The Ledger

Export → Poshmark Listings (TXT) produces formatted, copy-paste-ready listing copy with title, SKU, brand, size, color, condition, price, description, and tags.

Quick tips

  • Cover-shot quality matters most — neutral background, daylight.
  • Share your closet daily (yours and others') — share-for-share is the discovery currency.
  • Offer a bundle discount to encourage multi-item purchases.
  • "Posh Parties" — themed time-windowed events you can list to. Watch the calendar.
  • Title gets cropped at 50 chars in feed — front-load brand and key keywords.

Mercari

Fast-turnover, anything

Fixed-price-only marketplace with simple buyer protections. Good for items that have a defined market price and need to move quickly. Lower fees than eBay; less reach.

Title
80 chars
Description
1000 chars
Photos
Up to 12
Fees
10% selling fee + 2.9% + $0.50 payment processing

From The Ledger

No direct export — copy fields from the editor: Listing Title, Listing Description, Price, brand, condition. Mercari has its own category picker; map your Ledger category there manually.

Quick tips

  • "Smart Pricing" auto-discounts a listing over time — useful for slow movers; set a sensible floor.
  • Free shipping ($9-and-up items) ranks higher than buyer-paid.
  • Mercari Authenticate available for high-value items (Beanies under $200 generally not eligible).
  • Bundle multiple items into a Mercari Shop listing for adjacent buyers.

Depop

Young, streetwear / vintage

Owned by Etsy, skews Gen Z, visual-first. Strong for vintage clothing, Y2K-era pieces, streetwear, and quirky collectibles. Hashtag-driven discovery.

Caption
~2200 chars (title is part of caption)
Photos
4 max
Fees
10% selling fee

From The Ledger

No direct export — copy Listing Description into the caption. Add hashtags (Depop's #y2k #vintage #beaniebaby etc. drive most of the discovery).

Quick tips

  • Photo aesthetic matters more than anywhere else — flat-lay or worn shots, consistent style across your shop.
  • Drop 5-10 relevant hashtags in the caption.
  • Follow + like-back is the etiquette.

Etsy

Vintage 20+ years, handcrafted

Best for items 20+ years old (most Beanie Babies qualify), handcrafted goods, or craft supplies. SEO-heavy — long-tail keyword matching is the whole game.

Title
140 chars
Photos
10 max
Fees
$0.20 insertion (4-mo) + 6.5% transaction + ~3% payment processing
Tags
13 tags allowed — use all of them

From The Ledger

Copy Listing Title and Listing Description. Set Etsy category to "Vintage" if item is 20+ years old. The Ledger's tags field maps directly to Etsy tags — fill all 13.

Quick tips

  • Pack long-tail keywords in the title — "Vintage 1997 Ty Princess Diana Beanie Baby PE Pellets Mint".
  • Tags should be 2-3 word phrases, not single words.
  • "Vintage" is a strict Etsy requirement — items must be 20+ years old. They actively police it.
  • Renewal at month 4 is $0.20 again — factor that into pricing if you expect slow sales.

Universal tips for any online listing

  • Photograph in daylight. Natural light makes plush look plush, color look accurate, and tags readable.
  • Write the title last. Once you know the description, pick the strongest keywords for the title.
  • List honestly. "NWT" / "NWOT" / condition grades have real meanings — buyer claims are won and lost on accuracy.
  • Cross-list, but track it. The Ledger's status field handles "Listed - Both" for items on more than one platform. Pull from the unsold platform fast when one sells.
  • Pack the same day. Most platforms reward fast shipping with placement bumps.

Facebook Marketplace

Largest local audience

Local pickup is the dominant mode — no fees, no shipping logistics, cash in hand. Best for furniture, electronics, household goods, lots, and bulk Beanie collections. Shipping is also available but most listings stay local.

Title
~100 char recommended
Photos
10 max
Fees
Free for local pickup; ~5% for shipped items
Pickup radius
You set the area; ~25mi typical

From The Ledger

Copy Listing Title, Listing Description, and Price. Set Facebook category to "Toys & Games" or similar. Bulk lots work great here — list a whole Beanie collection as one item rather than 50 separate listings.

Quick tips

  • Respond within 30 minutes if you can — buyer ghosting is the norm, fast response wins.
  • Public meet-up spots: police-station parking lots are common; many stations have designated areas.
  • Mark "Sold" promptly — Marketplace re-shows old listings otherwise.
  • Cash or PayPal F&F only — no Venmo to strangers, no Zelle (no buyer protection).
  • Use the "Boost" sparingly — most local sales come from search, not paid placement.

Craigslist

Classic local, free, no account needed

Old-school but still huge in major metros. Best for furniture, lots, bulk inventory, and odd items that don't fit other categories. Zero fees, no account required (though one helps with managing posts).

Title
70 chars
Photos
24 max
Fees
Free for personal sales
Post duration
~30 days; re-post every 3 days for visibility

From The Ledger

Copy Listing Title, Listing Description, Price. Set Craigslist category — Collectibles or "for sale: by owner" for general items. The Ledger's description carries over but the formatting is plain-text only.

Quick tips

  • Always use the email-relay (anonymous) address Craigslist generates. Never paste your real email.
  • Meet in public places, daylight, cash only. Avoid checks and money orders.
  • Re-post every ~3 days; listings older than a few days get buried under new ones.
  • Use "delete" when sold — Craigslist doesn't auto-mark as sold like other platforms.
  • Common scam: "I'll have my mover pick it up, can I send a check?" → no.

OfferUp

Local-first, app-native

Local-pickup-leaning marketplace with in-app messaging and trust scores. Skews younger / mobile than Craigslist. Shipping is supported for small items but most listings are local-only.

Title
50 chars
Photos
12 max
Fees
Free for local pickup; 12.9% (min $1.99) on shipped items

From The Ledger

Copy Listing Title, Listing Description, Price. Set "Local pickup" if you don't want to ship — that removes the marketplace fee entirely.

Quick tips

  • Verify your account (phone, email, ID) — TruYou badge boosts buyer trust significantly.
  • "Promote" costs OfferUp credits but isn't usually worth it for low-priced items.
  • In-app messaging only — never give out your phone number or move chat off-platform.
  • Meet at "CommunityMeetUp" verified spots if available in your area.

Nextdoor

Neighborhood-only, trust-based

Hyper-local — only your verified neighborhood (and nearby) can see the listing. Lower volume than other local platforms but higher trust. Good for items that benefit from face-to-face vouching: moving sales, kid toys, garage-sale lots.

Format
Posts in "Sell or give away" section
Photos
10 max
Fees
Free
Reach
Your neighborhood + ~5 nearby

From The Ledger

Copy Listing Title, Listing Description, Price. Or bundle multiple items as a single "garage sale" listing with a Saturday date.

Quick tips

  • Verified-by-real-address means lower spam and ghosting risk than other local platforms.
  • Best for items where buyers want to inspect first (kids' toys, larger collectibles).
  • Garage-sale-style multi-item listings get more engagement than single items.

Universal tips for local pickup

  • Meet in public, daylight, cash only. Police-station parking lots are ideal — many designate "exchange zones."
  • Don't share your home address until you trust the buyer. Use a nearby landmark for the meet.
  • Bring exact change. "I forgot my wallet" / "I only have a hundred" is a classic stall tactic.
  • Hold the item for buyer #1 for 24 hours max. If they ghost, sell to buyer #2 without guilt.
  • Cash is king for local. Avoid Venmo to strangers, no Zelle (no buyer protection), no checks, no PayPal Goods & Services for in-person (it's for shipping).
  • Multi-item garage sale often beats individual listings for low-value lots — one weekend, dozens sold.
