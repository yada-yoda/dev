🐾 PawPrints

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

PawPrints is a small, personal, non-commercial app for keeping track of a dog's daily care — feeding, water, potty trips, walks, vet visits, grooming, meds, weight, and supplies. This page explains, plainly, what it stores and who it talks to. There are no ads, no trackers sold to anyone, and nothing about your dog is ever sold or shared for marketing.

Signing in

You sign in with Google Sign-In (through Google Firebase Authentication). When you do, PawPrints receives your Google account's email address and a unique user ID. These are used only to save your data to your own account and to sync it across your devices — nothing else. You can use the app signed out, in which case data stays only in your browser.

What's stored, and where

The things you enter — your dog's profile and photo, and your logs (feeding, water, potty, walks, weight), vet visits, grooming, medications, illnesses, supplies, contacts, and settings — are stored:

  • In your browser's local storage, so the app works offline; and
  • When signed in, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account, so it syncs across devices.

Photos are stored inline with your data (no separate image host). Your dog's data is kept in a separate record from any other app that shares the same Google login.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, PawPrints can add and update calendar events for vet visits, grooming, follow-ups, and reminders. To keep you connected without re-asking every visit, a small helper service stores a Google refresh token on the server side (Cloudflare KV) — it never reaches the browser and is used only to talk to your calendar. You can disconnect at any time from the Calendar tab, which revokes and deletes that token. If you never connect Calendar, none of this applies.

Weather on walks (optional)

If you set a home ZIP code for walk weather, PawPrints sends that ZIP (and the latitude/longitude it resolves to) to zippopotam.us and Open-Meteo to look up the weather. Only location coordinates are sent — never your name, your dog, or anything identifying. If you don't set a ZIP, no weather lookups happen.

Analytics

PawPrints uses Google Analytics 4 G-5R71TRBEQW to understand basic, anonymous usage — how many visits there are and which sections (tabs) get used. IP addresses are anonymized, and no personal data is sent (no dog names, no health details, no account info). Analytics is turned off entirely in the demo (?demo=1), and you can opt any device out by visiting the app with ?noga=1 (re-enable with ?noga=0). Google Analytics sets its own cookies; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing access

If you invite someone (a family member, sitter, or dog walker) to help, the email address you invite is stored with your data so the app can grant them access at the level you choose. You can remove their access at any time in Settings.

Your control

  • Export everything as a JSON backup (or spreadsheet/PDF) from Settings, any time.
  • Delete data within the app; signed-in deletions remove it from Firestore. Clearing your browser data removes the local copy.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar to revoke that access.

Children & scope

PawPrints is a personal tool for tracking pets, not a service aimed at children, and it isn't intended to collect data about people beyond the optional collaborator emails described above.

Contact

This is a personal project. For privacy questions, reach the maintainer on GitHub at github.com/itsavibecode. If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it.
