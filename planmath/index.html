Your plans

Everything here is on your plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) sheet. Costs marked "after deductible" are charged at full price until the deductible is met — the simulation handles that month by month.

Your usage

Applies to every plan. Prescriptions can sit in different tiers under different plans — set the tier per plan when adding one.

Results

Click any column header to sort. Worst-case year = premiums + out-of-pocket max − HSA/HRA money.

Plan years — predicted vs. actual

One row per plan year you actually lived through. Log what you really paid and compare it to what the estimate predicted, so next year's decision gets smarter.

Saved comparisons

Snapshots of past comparisons — when they were made, with whom, and the full plan set, which you can load back into the Compare tab any time.
