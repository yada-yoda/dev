Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

PlanMath works in two ways, and you choose which. The short version: signed out, your data stays in your browser. Signed in with Google, it is saved to your Google account so you can open it in any browser. Either way there are no analytics, no ads, and nothing is sold.

Signed out: your data stays on your device

If you never sign in, everything you enter (plans, premiums, prescriptions, doctor-visit counts, plan-year history, saved comparisons) is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Signed in: your data is saved to your Google account

Signing in is optional. When you sign in with Google, your plans, prescriptions, visit counts, plan-year history and saved comparisons are stored in Google Firebase (a Google database service called Firestore), filed under your Google account's ID. A security rule lets only your signed-in account read or change that record. Google provides the sign-in and hosts the data. The site operator administers that database, so the operator has technical access to what is stored there. Your name, email address and profile picture come from Google to show who is signed in.

While you are signed in, a working copy is also kept in this browser so the app still works if the connection drops. Signing out removes that copy, unless some changes have not reached your account yet. Then they stay on the device until you sign in again and they finish saving.

If you had data in this browser before signing in, PlanMath moves it into your account the first time you sign in, and removes the browser-only copy once your account has it. If your account already has data, it asks you which to keep.

Google's sign-in scripts

Every visit loads Google's Firebase sign-in scripts, even when you never sign in, so that the Sign in button can work. Loading them means Google's servers see a standard request from your browser. The database script loads only after you sign in.

No analytics, no ads, no trackers

PlanMath contains no analytics scripts, no advertising, and no social widgets. Nothing you enter is sold or shared for marketing. PlanMath itself sets no cookies; if you sign in, Google's sign-in keeps you signed in using your browser's storage.

Export, import, and printing are local

The Export button creates a JSON file directly in your browser and saves it to your device. Import reads a file you choose from your device; it is never uploaded. Print / PDF uses your browser's own print engine. In every case the data moves only between your browser and your own files.

Hosting

The site's static files are served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. Like virtually all web hosts, those providers may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of request) to operate their networks. That logging is theirs, not ours. The hosting itself receives none of the data you enter. See GitHub's privacy documentation and Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle logs.

Deleting your data

Signed out: clearing this site's data in your browser settings erases it completely. That also means it can't be recovered for you, so use Export if you want a backup.

Signed in: delete your plans, prescriptions, plan years and saved comparisons in the app. Each change overwrites the copy saved in your account. Then sign out, which removes the copy kept in this browser.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be described in the project changelog and the date above will be updated.
