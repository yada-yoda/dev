Privacy

Last updated July 7, 2026

PlanMath is built so there is almost nothing to have a privacy policy about. The short version: your data stays in your browser, and this site collects nothing.

Your data never leaves your device

Everything you enter — plans, premiums, prescriptions, doctor-visit counts, plan-year history, saved comparisons — is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. There is no server-side database, no account, no sign-up, and no code on this site that transmits what you type anywhere.

No analytics, no cookies, no trackers

PlanMath contains no analytics scripts, no advertising, no social widgets, and sets no cookies. It loads no third-party scripts at all — the entire app is a single self-contained page.

Export, import, and printing are local

The Export button creates a JSON file directly in your browser and saves it to your device. Import reads a file you choose from your device; it is never uploaded. Print / PDF uses your browser's own print engine. In every case the data moves only between your browser and your own files.

Hosting

The site's static files are served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. Like virtually all web hosts, those providers may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of request) to operate their networks. That logging is theirs, not ours — PlanMath itself receives and stores nothing. See GitHub's privacy documentation and Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle logs.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives in your browser, clearing this site's data in your browser settings erases it completely. That also means we can't recover it for you — use Export if you want a backup.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be described in the project changelog and the date above will be updated.
