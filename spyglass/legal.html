Privacy Policy

Spyglass is a small, invite-only, personal web-page-change monitor. It collects the minimum needed to do that job and nothing else.

What's stored

  • Your Google account basics — name, email address, and avatar, provided by Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication). Used to identify your account and to send your alert emails.
  • Your monitors — the URLs you watch and their settings (selectors, keywords, patterns, check frequency).
  • Snapshots — the extracted text of monitored pages at each detected change, and (if enabled in Settings) a screenshot of the page at that moment.
  • Preferences — e.g. whether screenshots are enabled.

Where it lives & who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore) stores accounts and all data above. Access is restricted per-account by security rules — no user can read another user's data.
  • Cloudflare Workers performs the page checks and takes screenshots (Cloudflare Browser Rendering).
  • Resend delivers alert emails to your address.
  • r.jina.ai (a third-party page-rendering service) may be used to read JavaScript-heavy pages; monitored page URLs pass through it, but none of your account data does.

What's NOT here

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no data sale — ever.
  • No cookies beyond Google's own sign-in session; localStorage holds only cosmetic state (like which "What's new" note you've seen).

Deletion

Deleting a monitor in the app permanently deletes it and its whole snapshot history. To delete your entire account and data, email spyglass@stocks.bookhockeys.com.

Terms of Use

  • Spyglass is a personal, invite-only tool provided free of charge, as-is, with no uptime, accuracy, or availability guarantees. It may change or shut down at any time.
  • Use it only to monitor pages you may lawfully access. You are responsible for respecting the terms of service of the sites you monitor.
  • Don't use it to overload anyone's website (check frequencies are capped for this reason), to collect personal data about others, or for anything unlawful.
  • Accounts that abuse the service can be removed without notice.

Disclaimer

  • Monitoring is best-effort. Websites block automated checks, restructure their pages, and render content in ways that can make a check fail or miss a change. Never rely on Spyglass as your only signal for anything important.
  • Nothing here is financial advice. Monitors that watch interest rates, prices, or other financial figures simply report what a page displayed at check time — always verify with the institution before acting.
